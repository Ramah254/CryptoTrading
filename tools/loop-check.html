Before you try a loop

  • Kenya's DCI asked Binance to freeze accounts in April 2026. Some P2P users waited more than two months for access.
  • Raenest can quarantine an incoming stablecoin transfer it considers suspicious. It then stays out of your USD balance until cleared.
  • Kenya repealed its 3% Digital Asset Tax in 2025. Exchanges must now file annual user reports with KRA, and trading profit may count as taxable income. Ask a Kenyan tax adviser before you scale anything.

Fee sources: Safaricom send-money tariff, Raenest help centre (Kenya), PayPal to M-Pesa service, Binance withdrawal fees. P2P prices move about 0.05% in half an hour, so enter what you see now.
